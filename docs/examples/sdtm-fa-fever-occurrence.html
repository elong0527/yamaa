What this example means

Reactogenicity temperature records from VS produce one FA fever-occurrence record at the source-record grain:

  • FASEQ preserves the collected VS sequence number and its row identity;
  • FATESTCD, FATEST, FACAT, FASCAT, and FAOBJ identify a systemic reactogenicity fever occurrence;
  • FAORRES is Y when the Celsius result is 38 or higher, N when it is lower, and missing when the numeric result is missing or its unit is not Celsius;
  • FASTRESC matches FAORRES;
  • VSSTRESN preserves the numeric result used for the threshold.

This covers the upstream branch in which no fever FA record already exists.
